Screen Time History

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Screen Time History keeps and shows your own Screen Time history. It is built so that your data stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your data is

On your devices and in your own iCloud (your private iCloud database). Only you can access it. We, the developer, cannot read it.

Sharing

We don't share, sell or receive your data.

Deleting your data

Remove Screen Time History's data from your iCloud storage in your device's iCloud settings, and delete the apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
